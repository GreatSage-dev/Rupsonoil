Industrial Derivatives

Product Catalog

All derivatives meet stringent technical parameters. Available in bulk road tankers, 200L steel drums, and IBC totes.

Palm Stearin Oil

Solid fraction from palm oil fractionation at controlled temperatures. High melting point with optimal stability for soap, candle, and oleochemical production.

Bulk Tankers • 200L Drums PS-01

Crude Palm Kernel Oil (CPKO)

Unrefined oil extracted from the palm kernel nut, rich in lauric acid. Essential feedstock for high-lather soap making, surfactants, and cosmetic formulations.

Bulk Tankers • 200L Drums • IBC CPKO-02

Palm Fatty Acid Distillate (PFAD)

By-product from crude palm oil refining with high FFA concentrations. Cost-effective feedstock for laundry soap, animal feed formulations, and oleochemicals.

Bulk Tankers • 200L Drums PFAD-03

Palm Kernel Distilled Fatty Acid (PKDFA)

Distilled fatty acid fraction from palm kernel processing. High purity, light color, and concentrated fatty acid chains for premium cosmetics and toilet soaps.

Bulk Tankers • Sealed Drums PKDFA-04

Refined Bleached Deodorized Palm Oil (RBDPO)

Fully processed, refined palm oil with neutralized odor and reduced color index. High oxidative stability for food manufacturing, confectioneries, and bakery fats.

Bulk Tankers • Food-Grade Drums RBDPO-05
